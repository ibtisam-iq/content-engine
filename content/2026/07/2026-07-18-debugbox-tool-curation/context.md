# Canonical Strategic Brief

## 1. Technical Event Context

- **Event Description**: DebugBox v1.1.0 tool curation: 5 packages removed entirely, 4 moved from balanced to power, 1 moved from power to balanced (openssl), wget removed from balanced. Result: balanced -4 MB, power -21 MB.
- **Why It Matters**: Demonstrates engineering judgment in open-source maintenance. Removing tools from a released project is harder than adding them - it requires understanding user dependency, defining scope, and making the trade-offs explicit.

## 2. Target Audience Segments

- **Primary Audience**: Staff engineers and platform architects who maintain internal tooling or open-source projects.
- **Secondary Audience**: Engineering managers evaluating candidates on system design and product thinking.

## 3. Core Messaging Thesis

- **Primary Thesis**: Deliberate removal is harder than addition, and more valuable as a signal of engineering judgment.
- **Supporting Arguments**:
  - Argument 1: The evaluation framework (two questions) provides a replicable decision process, not a one-off judgment call.
  - Argument 2: Concrete results (51 to 47 MB, 112 to 91 MB) make the trade-off measurable.
  - Argument 3: The harder lesson is that every removal is a public statement about what the project is and is not.

## 4. Tone and Editorial Constraints

- **Tone**: Reflective, experience-driven, honest about the difficulty of removal decisions.
- **Constraints**: No em dashes. No banned buzzwords. Specific tool names and numbers throughout.
